## Outlier scan (IQR method, 1.5×IQR fences)

Zero outliers flagged by the IQR rule on any of the 5 features, in either train or test
(see `phase1_eda_results.json → outliers_iqr` for exact fence values). This is expected for
what looks like a designed/randomized sampling grid (e.g. Latin hypercube or uniform
random draws over fixed operating ranges) rather than field-collected plant data — there is
no long right/left tail to trigger the fences. This does **not** mean there are no
"extreme" *operating conditions* — see the zero-yield analysis, where the extremity is in
the *combination* of features (long residence + strong net heating), not any single
feature's marginal distribution.
